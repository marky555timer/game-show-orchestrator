Rounds

A trivia round ends when a team reaches its goal, or when its songs run out (then the leader on points wins). "Wait, how much?" asks price questions back to back (the playlist plays on underneath) until its question count or a team reaches its goal; Fastest Finger is one medley with its true/false game (most points wins); Tipsy Simon and Cliffhanger are solo challenges: one team, all or nothing (Cliffhanger opens straight into its title and the host's pregame screen). Every round is followed by an intermission. No rounds listed = standard trivia rounds, one after another, until STOP GAME. Blank goal / songs = the defaults shown. Each trivia / "Wait, how much?" round can have its own decade: its songs come from it and its wheel spin lands on it.
