What do the colours mean?
Grey — the song has fewer than 3 questions; they need to be generated or written.
Deep amber — the AI audit found an error and corrected it (or couldn't confirm a fact). Needs deep verification or your own research.
Red — questions exist but nobody has checked them. Possible errors.
Yellow-green — a bigger AI answered every question blind and agreed. Check it yourself if you wish.
Green — you verified every question.
A song shows the colour of its most urgent question. Anything you edit by hand drops back to red until you verify it.
